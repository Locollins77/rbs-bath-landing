Questions People Ask Before They Call

How Much Does a Walk-In Shower or Tub Cost?

It depends on the size of the space, which product you pick and the options you add, like seats, grab bars and wall patterns. That's why we measure first and give you a firm, written price at the visit instead of a guess over the phone. Your written retail price is good for one year, and special financing options are available, subject to credit approval.

Will It Really Be Done in One Day?

Most installs are finished in a day, often in 6 to 8 hours. About 1 in 20 needs a second day, usually because water damage or a soft subfloor shows up once the old tub is out. If that happens, we'll tell you right then. Either way, plan on 24 hours for the new shower to cure before you use it.

Will It Fit My Bathroom?

Almost always. Walk-in showers and conversions are made to fit the space your current tub or shower takes up, and we measure everything at the free visit before you commit to anything.

Do You Install Wheelchair-Accessible Showers?

Yes. For most people a low-threshold shower is the right fit: a short, easy step instead of a tall tub wall. If someone uses a wheelchair or a rolling walker, we also install barrier-free showers with no threshold at all. We'll help you decide at the in-home visit.

It's for My Mom or Dad. Can I Be There?

Yes, and we recommend it. Tell us when we call and we'll set the visit at their home at a time you can both be there, in person or on the phone.

Is the In-Home Visit Really Free? Will I Get a Hard Sell?

It's free, with no obligation. Plan on 60 to 90 minutes, including a virtual design tour where you see your new shower before you decide. You get a written price that's good for one year, and then we leave so you can think it over. Plenty of people take a few days or talk it over with family first, and that's fine.

What About Financing?

Special financing options are available, subject to credit approval. Your consultant can walk you through the plans at the visit.

What's the Warranty?

Your new system comes with a written warranty, and your consultant will go over exactly what's covered for the products you choose before you sign anything.

Do You Cover My Area?

We cover Northwest Lower Michigan, including Traverse City, Petoskey, Charlevoix, Gaylord, Cheboygan, Cadillac and Manistee, plus Chippewa, Mackinac and Luce counties in the Eastern U.P. Enter your zip in the form and we'll confirm.
